About

Who I am, where I studied, what I do outside class.

I’m Maryam, a Computer Science and Engineering student at Daffodil International University, now in my second year. I’m passionate about programming, and I’m especially interested in artificial intelligence and web development.

I write C, C++ and Java for my courses, and I enjoy turning a small idea into a program that works. My goal is to keep growing as a developer and, one day, to use what I learn to make a positive impact.

Away from the keyboard I garden and I love to travel. Gardening brings me closer to nature and teaches me patience, responsibility and how to nurture life. Travelling lets me explore new places and cultures and collect memories.
