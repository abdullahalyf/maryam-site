C is one of the languages I am learning at university. It is not the easiest one, but it teaches a lot.

## What feels hard

- **Details matter.** A missing semicolon or a wrong format specifier can stop a whole program.
- **Pointers.** They take time to understand. Drawing the variables and arrows on paper helped me more than rereading the book.
- **Debugging.** At first I changed things at random. Now I print values and check them one step at a time.

## What feels rewarding

Writing a small program, such as a result calculator that reads marks and prints grades and a GPA, and watching it work for the first time is a good feeling.

## What helps me

- Practise a little every day instead of cramming before a lab.
- Type the code myself, even when I understand the example.
- Test with simple numbers first, then with strange ones.

## What next

I am also learning C++ and Java, and I want to explore web development and AI. C is a solid base to build on.
