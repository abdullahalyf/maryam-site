Every season in Bangladesh has its own flower. These four are the ones I love most.

## Shapla (শাপলা)

The water lily, our national flower. It floats on ponds and still water in the rainy months, with its petals open above the green leaves.

## Krishnochura (কৃষ্ণচূড়া)

A tree that turns red and orange in the summer heat. Walking under it when it is in bloom feels like walking under a roof of flames.

## Shiuli (শিউলি)

A small, sweet-smelling white flower with an orange centre. It blooms in autumn and falls in the early morning, so the ground below the tree is covered with petals.

## Kadam (কদম)

A round, ball-shaped flower that belongs to the monsoon. It is one of the first things I think of when the rains arrive.

## Why they matter to me

Gardening made me notice flowers more. Now I like to know when each one blooms and what season it belongs to.
