Travelling is one of the things I enjoy most, and Cox's Bazar is one of the places I most want to see and remember.

## The sea beach

Cox's Bazar is known for one of the longest unbroken sea beaches in the world. I like the idea of a beach where you can keep walking, with the sea on one side and the sky open above.

## What I would look for

- Sunrise and sunset by the water
- Waves, rocks and quiet corners away from the crowd
- Local food and the people who live there

## Why I like places like this

For me, travelling is about exploring new places and cultures and creating memories that stay long after the trip. A place like this is a good reminder to slow down and look around.
