You do not need a big garden to grow flowers. A balcony with a few hours of sun and some pots is enough. These four are good places to start.

## Marigold (গাঁদা)

Bright orange and yellow flowers, and easy to grow from seed. It likes plenty of sun and does not need much fuss.

## Hibiscus (জবা)

A strong, cheerful flower. Give it a big pot, sun and regular water, and it will keep blooming for a long time.

## Beli (বেলি)

A small white jasmine with a sweet smell, lovely on an evening balcony. Keep it in a pot with good drainage.

## Rose

Roses ask for more care than the others. They need good sunlight, regular watering and a pot that drains well, but a fresh bloom makes the effort worthwhile.

## A few simple rules

- Pick the sunniest spot you have.
- Use pots with holes so water does not collect at the bottom.
- Water when the top of the soil feels dry, not on a fixed timetable.
- Start with one or two plants, and add more when those are happy.
