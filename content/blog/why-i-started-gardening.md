Between lectures, labs and screens, gardening is the part of my week that moves at the speed of a plant. That is exactly why I love it.

## It brings me closer to nature

Holding soil, checking a new leaf and watching a bud open is a small reminder that the world is bigger than my laptop screen.

## It teaches patience

A seed does not care about my deadlines. You water it, give it light and wait. Some days nothing seems to happen, and then one morning there is a new shoot.

## It teaches responsibility

A plant cannot ask for water. If I forget, it suffers. Looking after something that depends on me is a good habit to carry into everything else.

## Nurturing life

Helping something grow is a quiet kind of happiness. I think it also makes me a more careful student, because programming needs the same patience: small steps, regular attention and a lot of trying again.
