If I had to pick one landscape in Bangladesh to walk through slowly, it would be a tea garden in the Sylhet region.

## A different kind of green

Tea gardens are made of low, trimmed bushes planted in rows over gentle hills, with tall shade trees standing among them. Srimangal is often called the tea capital of Bangladesh.

## Why it appeals to me

I spend a lot of time looking at screens, and a view like this is the opposite: wide, green and quiet. As someone who likes gardening, I also enjoy seeing plants grown carefully on such a large scale.

## What I like to do there

- Walk between the rows in the morning
- Learn how tea leaves are picked and made
- Drink a cup of fresh tea with the hills in front of me
